# Drum and bass (general)

Covers rollers, neurofunk, jump-up, dancefloor/"jungle-tinged" and minimal DnB. For chord-led liquid/atmospheric styles use `jazzy-dnb.md`.

**Tempo**: 170–176 BPM; harmony moves at half-time.

## Harmonic palette

- Often minimal: one minor key, the bass riff *is* the harmony. Aeolian and phrygian (b2) dominate the darker subgenres; harmonic minor for drama.
- Power chords and octaves, minor triads, sus2/sus4, stacked 5ths for stabs. Extensions sparingly — the reese and mid-bass already fill the spectrum.
- Tension through the b2 (phrygian), the tritone, chromatic bass movement, and pedal notes rather than chord changes.
- Dancefloor/anthemic: i–bVI–bIII–bVII (`Fm Db Ab Eb`), i–bVII–bVI–bVII; big supersaw chords with octaves.

## Progression shapes

- `Fm Gb` — i–bII phrygian swing (neuro/darkside).
- `Fm Db Ab Eb` — anthemic minor loop.
- `Fm` pedal with `Fm Gbmaj7/F Ebm/F Fm` above it — moving upper chords over a fixed sub.
- `Em C G D` → `Em9 Cmaj7 G6 Dsus4` when it needs to warm up (bridge towards liquid).

## Bass relationship

- The mid-bass riff carries rhythm and movement; the sub carries roots. Riffs: root, b2, b3, 5, b7, octave, with slides.
- Keep chords away from the bass register entirely (pads above C3/60 in busy sections).
- Use `validate --role bass` to check the riff's downbeats against any chords you add.

## Rhythm

- Two-step drums: kick on 1 and the "and" of 3 (`--hits` positions 0 and 2.5), snare on 2 and 4 (positions 1 and 3). Bass and stabs work around the snare.
- Stabs: short, one per bar or on syncopated 16ths; harmonic rhythm 1–4 bars.

## Arrangement

16–32-bar DJ intro/outro, drop at bar 33 or 65, breakdown with atmospheric pads (borrow from `jazzy-dnb.md`), second drop often with a switched-up bass riff. Variation by changing the riff every 8–16 bars rather than the chords.

## Pitfalls

Too many chord changes during the drop; chords with 3rds in the low-mids; tonal clashes between the riff's notes and the pad's extensions (check with `validate`).
