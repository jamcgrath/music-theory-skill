# Downtempo (trip-hop, chillout, ambient-leaning)

Touchpoints: Bristol trip-hop (Massive Attack, Portishead, Tricky), chillout/ambient (Boards of Canada, Air, Thievery Corporation's dub side). Stylistic references only.

**Tempo**: 70–110 BPM; trip-hop often 80–95 with heavy, swung or dragging drums.

## Harmonic palette

- **Trip-hop**: minor keys, harmonic minor and its V7 (`Gm Gm(maj7) Gm7 Gm6` line clichés, `Cm Ab G7`), string-pad drama, chromatic descending lines, b6 and b2 colours, film-noir minor 6/9 and m(maj7).
- **Chillout/ambient**: lydian and mixolydian colours, maj7/add9/sus2, slow harmonic rhythm (2–8 bars per chord), drones and pedals, quartal pads.
- Ambiguity is a feature: chords without 3rds (sus2, 5ths) let the melody decide major or minor.

## Progression shapes

- `Gm Gm(maj7) Gm7 Gm6` — descending line cliché (one inner voice moving down by semitones).
- `Cm Ab G7` — minor i–bVI–V with harmonic-minor pull.
- `Fmaj7#11 G6/F` — lydian vamp over an F pedal.
- `Dsus2 Bm7add11 Gmaj9 Asus4` — floating, 3rd-light major.
- `Am(maj9) Fmaj7#11 Dm9 E7b9` — dark cinematic minor loop.

## Voicing and instruments

Strings, pads, Rhodes, guitars, sampled vinyl loops. Wide pad voicings, sparse keys. Trip-hop strings: close triads with one moving inner line. Ambient: open 5ths/9ths, long notes, slow attack.

## Bass relationship

Trip-hop: heavy, sustained, often dub-like with space; bass may hold while chords descend (line cliché over a fixed root). Chillout: soft sub or no bass at all in sections.

## Rhythm and melody

Slow, swung or behind-the-beat. Melodies: breathy, repetitive, narrow range; minor pentatonic and harmonic minor; long notes with expressive pitch bends.

## Arrangement

Song form or slowly evolving loops; textural changes over harmonic ones. Breakdowns via subtraction.

## Pitfalls

Over-busy harmonic rhythm; extensions that make trip-hop sound like smooth jazz (keep it darker and simpler); muddy low-mid pads under a heavy bass.
