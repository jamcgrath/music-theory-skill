# House (deep, soulful, garage-adjacent, piano house)

Touchpoints: Chicago/New Jersey deep house (Larry Heard, Kerri Chandler), soulful/garage house (Masters at Work, Blaze), 90s piano house, modern deep house. Stylistic references only.

**Tempo**: 118–128 BPM, four-on-the-floor.

## Harmonic palette

- **Deep house**: m7, m9, m11 stabs; "chord memory" — one stab shape transposed to new roots (planing), so a whole track is one colour on different roots.
- **Soulful/garage**: gospel progressions, maj7/9, slash chords (`F/G`, `Ab/Bb`), passing diminished, IV–iii–ii–I descents.
- **Piano house**: bright triads and 7ths, major keys, I–V–vi–IV family with suspensions.
- Two-chord loops are standard; four-chord loops for vocal tracks.

## Progression shapes

- `Cm9 Fm9` — deep-house i–iv stab loop.
- `Fm11 Ebm11 Dbm11 Ebm11` — one m11 shape planed (chord-memory moves).
- `Fmaj9 Em7 Dm9 Cmaj9` — IV–iii–ii–I soulful descent.
- `Dm9 G9sus4 Cmaj9 Am9` — ii–V–I–vi with a sus dominant.
- `Bbmaj7 C/Bb Am7 Dm9` — garage/gospel lift over a held bass.

## Voicing and instruments

Stabs (3–4 notes, mid register, short) from organ/string-machine/synth patches; M1-style piano and organ; pads under vocals. Keep stab shapes identical when planing (`voice --style stab` per chord, or copy one shape and transpose all notes by the root difference — then `validate` the result against the new chord names).

## Bass relationship

Offbeat or syncopated bass (between kicks), root–octave patterns, organ bass. Bass stays on roots so planed chords keep their identity.

## Rhythm

Offbeat stab patterns (`--hits 0.5,1.5,2.5,3.5`), pushes (`--hits 0,0.75,1.5,2.5`), swing 54–60%. Harmonic rhythm 1–2 bars.

## Arrangement

Long intros/outros (16–32 bars) of drums and bass; introduce chords filtered; breakdown with pads and the full progression; vocal tracks in verse/chorus form over the same loop with variation via bass and top voice.

## Pitfalls

Stabs in the kick/bass register; too many chord changes for a dance loop; mismatched stab shapes when planing (breaks the chord-memory effect).
