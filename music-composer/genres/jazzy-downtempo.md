# Jazzy downtempo (nu-jazz, jazz-hop, chill/lounge jazz)

Touchpoints: St Germain-style deep jazz house at slower tempos, Nightmares on Wax, Kruder & Dorfmeister's dub-jazz, Bonobo's early records, jazz-hop/lo-fi beats. Stylistic references only.

**Tempo**: 80–110 BPM (jazz-hop 70–95). Swing and behind-the-beat feel matter more than tempo.

## Harmonic palette

- ii–V–I fragments and turnarounds (`Dm9 G13 Cmaj9 A7b13`), minor ii–V's, backdoor iv–bVII.
- m9, m11, 13, 7#9, 7b13, maj9, 6/9; altered dominants used as colour, not drama.
- Dorian vamps (`Em9 A13`), modal one-chord grooves with a moving bass.
- Chromatic passing chords and tritone subs (`Ebm9 D13 Dbmaj9` — the D13 slides into Db).
- Sampled-jazz feel: a 2–4 bar loop of 3–4 rich chords repeated, varied by the bass or the top note.

## Progression shapes

- `Dm9 G13 Cmaj9 A7b13` — ii–V–I with a VI7 turnaround (A7 is the V of Dm, so the loop pulls back to bar 1).
- `Ebm9 Ab13 Dbmaj9 Bb7#9` — the same in Db, darker with the #9.
- `Em9 A13` — dorian two-chord vamp.
- `Fm9 Bb13 Ebmaj9 Abmaj9` — ii–V–I–IV.
- `Cm9 Fm9 Bbm9 Eb13 Abmaj7 D7#9 G7alt` — longer minor chain with a pull back to C minor.

## Voicing and instruments

Rhodes and Wurlitzer (rootless, comped), upright or electric bass, horns (flugelhorn, sax), vibes, nylon guitar. Voicings mid-register, close, with 9ths and 13ths; piano/Rhodes two-hand voicings fit the slower tempo.

## Bass relationship

Melodic or walking bass, inversions welcome; upright-style slides and approach notes. Sub layers are lighter than in DnB. A pedal under a vamp works well for intros.

## Rhythm and melody

Swung 16ths (55–62%), lazy comping (`--hits 0,1.5,2.75`), anticipations. Melodies are short, bluesy, pentatonic with chromatic approach tones; horn-like phrasing with space.

## Arrangement

Intro (keys alone or with filtered drums) → groove → solo/feature section over the same changes → breakdown (bass + keys) → return. Song forms (AABA, 8-bar sections) work here.

## Pitfalls

Quantising everything stiff; dense voicings in the low register under a warm bass; alterations on every dominant (pick one moment).
